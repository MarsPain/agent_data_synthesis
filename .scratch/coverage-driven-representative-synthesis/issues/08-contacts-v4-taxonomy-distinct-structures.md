# 08 — Add Taxonomy-Distinct Contacts V4 Structures

**What to build:** Extend the contacts domain pack with a real search-and-select
capability and versioned v4 coverage structures that add independently
executable and verifiable structural families. Cover at least one read-only
search-to-lookup sequence and one state-changing
search-to-lookup-to-follow-up sequence without changing the shared scheduler or
counting instruction, grounding, or cell-name variation as structural growth.

**Blocked by:** None; informed by
[06 — Validate Representative Coverage Campaigns](06-representative-coverage-campaign.md)
and
[07 — Expand Representative Structural Catalogs](07-expand-representative-structural-catalogs.md)

**Status:** ready-for-agent

**Assignee:** Unassigned

**Parent spec:** [Coverage-Driven Representative Synthesis](../../../docs/product-specs/coverage-driven-representative-synthesis.md)

**Design context:** [Representative Structural Taxonomy](../../../docs/design-docs/representative-structural-taxonomy.md)

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The contacts environment and tool registry expose a deterministic,
  observation-backed search-and-select capability while preserving existing
  exact-lookup and follow-up behavior.
- [ ] The contacts generation specification, task contracts, scripted policy,
  and verifier execute at least one read-only search-to-lookup structure and
  one state-changing search-to-lookup-to-follow-up structure.
- [ ] The state-changing structure passes mutation authorization and argument
  provenance checks, binds the selected contact observation into later
  actions, and verifies the final follow-up state independently.
- [ ] New contacts v4 catalog, coverage-profile, and run-profile versions are
  registered without changing v1 through v3 identities, hashes, fixtures, or
  default behavior.
- [ ] Deterministic fake-provider runs classify every accepted v4 sample under
  the common structural taxonomy with zero unclassifiable samples and prove
  that target 30 fulfills at least one family absent at target 12.
- [ ] Tests pin each new cell's expected taxonomy features, retain the maximum
  two accepted samples per grounding unit, and fail if cell-count growth
  collapses entirely into families already fulfilled at the smaller target.
- [ ] Focused tests, the full unit suite, documentation validation, and retained
  material scans pass before any provider pilot is requested.

## Scope guard

Do not change the shared coverage scheduler or taxonomy merely to increase a
family count. Do not add redundant tool calls, unsupported mutations, arbitrary
fixture rows, instruction paraphrases, or higher grounding reuse as diversity.
Do not make paid provider calls without new explicit provider, model,
credential, and budget authorization.
